# Sources

Status: PENDING. Fill this in once, then link to it. Do not copy it into other files.

**Home:** the one place that lists what the project's claims rest on.

## What goes here

- One entry per source: title, author or owner, date, link or location, and the date you read it.
- A one-line note of what it supports, and how far you trust it (primary, secondary, opinion).
- Sources that conflict, side by side, with what you decided and why.
- Sources that are outdated, marked as such.

## One home per fact

- A finding that other work depends on is stated once, in `.memory/70-knowledge.md`, and cites an entry here.
- A decision made from the sources goes in `.memory/60-decisions.md`.
- Do not paste long quotations. Link to the source and quote only the line you rely on.
