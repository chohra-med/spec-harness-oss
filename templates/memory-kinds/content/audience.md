**Home:** the one place that says who the work is for and what they need.

## What goes here

- The reader groups, each in one or two sentences: who they are and what they want from this work.
- What each group already knows, and what you must explain.
- What each group should do or feel after reading.
- Where they meet the work (channel, device, moment).

## One home per fact

- How the work sounds lives in `voice-dna.md`; link to it.
- What the product is and why it exists lives in `.memory/10-product.md`.
- Evidence about the audience (surveys, interviews) lives in `sources.md` when this project keeps research; cite it here, do not paste it.
