# Voice DNA

Status: PENDING. Fill this in once, then link to it. Do not copy it into other files.

**Home:** the one place that says how this project's writing sounds. Every page, post or message is checked against this file.

## What goes here

- Who is speaking (a person, a team, a brand) and to whom, in one sentence.
- Tone words, with a short "sounds like / never sounds like" pair for each.
- Sentence habits: length, person (I, we), contractions, punctuation you avoid.
- Words you always use and words you never use.
- Two or three short passages you consider on voice, copied from approved work.

## One home per fact

- The audience itself lives in `audience.md`. Link to it; do not restate it.
- A decision to change the voice goes in `.memory/60-decisions.md`, with this file edited to match.
- Rules a reviewer must check on every piece (for example "no jargon") go in the project lens (`ai_rules/review-lens.md`), naming this file as the source.
