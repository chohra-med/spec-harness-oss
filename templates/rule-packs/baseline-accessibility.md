# Baseline pack: accessibility

<!-- rule-layer: baseline -->
<!-- pack: baseline-accessibility -->

- **A11Y-01** Give every interactive element a name, role and state that assistive technology can read. (source: WCAG 2.2, https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/, success criterion 4.1.2)
- **A11Y-02** Provide a text alternative for every meaningful image and hide decorative images from assistive technology. (source: WCAG 2.2, https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/, success criterion 1.1.1)
- **A11Y-03** Never use colour as the only way to convey information. (source: WCAG 2.2, https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/, success criterion 1.4.1)
- **A11Y-04** Keep normal text contrast at least 4.5 to 1 against its background. (source: WCAG 2.2, https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/, success criterion 1.4.3)
- **A11Y-05** Make touch and click targets at least 24 by 24 pixels or space them so they do not overlap. (source: WCAG 2.2, https://www.w3.org/TR/2023/REC-WCAG22-20231005/, success criterion 2.5.8)
