# Standard pack: EU accessibility (European Accessibility Act, EN 301 549)

<!-- rule-layer: standard -->
<!-- pack: standard-eu-accessibility -->
<!-- standard: European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882); harmonised standard EN 301 549 -->

Adopt this pack only when `.memory/10-product.md` records a commitment to it (a `STD-` line), and add
`<!-- commitment: STD-<n> -->` here. Whether the Act applies (market, product or service type,
microenterprise exemption) is a legal decision for the owner, not for init. Before each initialisation,
check which EN 301 549 version is harmonised now; this pack was checked on 2026-10-21.

- **EUACC-01** Meet every WCAG 2.1 level A and AA success criterion on every screen of the app. (source: European Commission, EN 301 549 harmonised standard v3.2.1 referencing WCAG 2.1, https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility-directive-standards-and-harmonisation)
- **EUACC-02** Give every custom control a name, role and state that VoiceOver and TalkBack announce. (source: European Commission, EN 301 549 harmonised standard v3.2.1 referencing WCAG 2.1, https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility-directive-standards-and-harmonisation)
- **EUACC-03** Follow the user's platform text size and accessibility settings instead of fixed sizes. (source: European Commission, EN 301 549 harmonised standard v3.2.1 referencing WCAG 2.1, https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility-directive-standards-and-harmonisation)
- **EUACC-04** Publish the service's accessibility information, saying how it meets the accessibility requirements. (source: Directive (EU) 2019/882 European Accessibility Act, https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eudr/2019/882/chapter/II)
