<!-- pack: baseline-design -->

- **DESIGN-01** Before adding a function, component, hook, type or constant, search the codebase for one that does the same job and reuse or extend it. (source: Spec Harness RFC 0012, owner decision 2026-11-02)
- **DESIGN-02** Reuse or extend the owning implementation for repeated behavior, or record a concrete reason for independently maintained similar code. (source: Spec Harness RFC 0012, owner decision 2026-11-02)
- **DESIGN-03** Keep related behavior cohesive and separate independently changing responsibilities when a concrete dependency or change scenario shows why. (source: Spec Harness RFC 0012, owner decision 2026-11-02)
